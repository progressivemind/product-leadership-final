The case

Five deliverables, one strategy

Each lab committed one decision. Together they are the bet, the sequence, the team that owns it, the money, and what you learned building it.

Module 1
Product strategy
Where you play and how you win

Winning Aspiration: The app is a true wellness companion that helps people overcome and monitor their anxiety acute.
Where to Play: Mozambican urban professionals, aged 40 to 65, experiencing recurring work-related anxiety that interferes with daily functioning.
How to Win: Identification of patterns, potential triggers, or contributing factors to anxiety scenarios and recommendations on how to avoid future cases, based on non-payable track history data.

The hard no

The hard no: We will not build sports tracking. We will rather integrate selectively with existing activity ecosystems where the data demonstrably improves personalization, because it’s a different business model and my lead to unprecedent outcomes and cost to our customers, given the complexity to integrate different personalization engines.

Objective and key results

Objective: Help users build and maintain healthier mental wellbeing by recognizing rising anxiety early and taking effective action.

KR1: Users who report that personalized interventions helped them manage an anxiety episode: >= 75% by Q1 2027.
KR2: Users who take a recommended intervention based on early identification of rising anxiety: >= 45% by Q2 2027.
KR3: Reduced recurrence of problematic anxiety episodes: 40% to 10% by Q4 2028.

Module 2
Outcome roadmap
Now, next, and later

Now: Bet on timely intervention: Enable users to reduce acute stress when it emerges by making personalized support available in the moments they need it most.; Wellbeing Product + AI/Personalization + UX/Design; Users entering high-stress states show a measurable reduction in stress/anxiety after engaging with an intervention; fewer users remain in acute/high-stress states.
Next: Predictive personalization: Use behavioral signals to anticipate rising stress and surface interventions before users reach an acute state.; AI/Personalization + Data/Analytics + Wellbeing Product;
Later: Interventions triggered by predicted risk reduce subsequent high-stress episodes versus baseline.

Trade-off memo

I chose to sequence timely intervention, personalized engagement, and accessibility first because they have the strongest combination of customer impact, strategic alignment, and urgency. Timely intervention directly targets KR2, while personalized engagement supports KR1 and establishes the behavioral data and learning needed for the later predictive-AI strategy.
I pushed out predictive personalization, the AI wellness companion, seamless intervention moments, adaptive wellness journeys, and proactive engagement because their value depends on foundations that are not yet sufficiently proven. Building sophisticated prediction or AI orchestration before establishing which interventions actually reduce stress risks increasing complexity without improving outcomes.
I cut eature-level commitments that do not represent a meaningful customer or business outcome entirely because they would turn the roadmap back into a delivery checklist. In particular, I would not treat “crisis mode flow,” “AI-generated check-in prompts,” or “WCAG compliance” as roadmap items in themselves. They remain delivery mechanisms within broader strategic bets. This keeps scarce capacity focused on changing user outcomes rather than maximizing feature output.

Module 5
Financial model
The business case

The $2 contribution and six-month incremental lifetime are assumptions that must be validated against Fable's actual ARPU, gross margin, and observed post-acute retention curve. The bet is expected to increase 90-day post-acute retention by 8 percentage points, creating 8 additional retained users for every 100 eligible post-acute users. Assuming each additional retained user generates $2 of incremental contribution per month and remains active for an average of 6 additional months, the bet generates $12 of incremental contribution per 100 eligible users, before incremental AI and operating costs. At 100,000 eligible post-acute users, this represents approximately 8,000 additional retained users and $120,000 of incremental contribution over the additional six-month lifetime, before personalization costs.

Kill criterion

Stop the bet if, after 90 days of reaching a statistically meaningful post-acute cohort, personalized check-ins do not produce at least a +5 percentage-point improvement in 90-day post-acute retention versus the control group.

Module 6

What I learned

Where the strategy was hardest to write, what you will keep doing, and the moment your thinking changed.

Friction

The hardest was on team charter because I had to reflect on my own behavior and think about how to overcome a real challenge with my team members. I tried to be more realistic and put something that I could consider in near future.

Key learnings

Communicate more on product strategies, mainly on outcomes rather on outputs only.

Aha moment

The simplicity of the used framework and tools. The line up on how product strategies can be built.
