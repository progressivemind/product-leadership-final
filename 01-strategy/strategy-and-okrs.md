# Craft an Advanced Product Strategy, Module 1 Lab

## Write your one-page strategy
- **Winning aspiration: define winning in the customer's terms, not internal metrics. What does success look like for the specific person you serve?:** Busy professionals don't have time for traditional wellness programs, so we help them recognize and regulate rising stress through interventions that fit naturally into their existing daily behavior without requiring manual tracking.
- **Where to play: segment, geography, and use case, explicitly. Which customers, markets, and surfaces will you focus on?:** Millennials who are eager to balance life and work, feel need to improve their mental wellbeing and do not have enough time to.
- **How to win: your differentiator. What can you do that your specific competitors cannot easily replicate?:** A plan for mental wellness based on current data and predictions linked to client's behavioral habits not manual mood tracking.
- **Capabilities required: what you must be world-class at. What will you build, buy, or partner for?:** User personalization engine that acts as AI companion
- **Management systems: the metrics and rituals that keep your choices alive quarter to quarter.:** Monthly retention review. User notification on abandoned features.

## Name your one hard no
- **Your one hard no:** We will not provide the ability for customers  to organize their content library based on most used features, because we want to build an immersive experience to our customers where the app will automatically organize the content.

## Write your three OKRs
- **Objective:** Become the app people trust to make their lives happier and protect their mental wellbeing.
- **KR1:** Number of users reporting improved mental health: 65% to 85% by Q1 2027
- **KR2:** Users experiencing acute/high stress or anxiety: 70% to 30% by Q2 2027
- **KR3:** Users repeating the same wellness program: 40% to 10% by Q4 2028

## AI pressure-test
- **Which challenge from the AI is most valid, and why?:** The alignment between the strategic objective and what the OKRs should be (more outcomes and not milestones)
- **What would you change based on the pushback, and what would you defend?:** I would redefine my objective aligning with the Winning Aspiration, making it concise and clear. I would defend the How to Win.
