# Craft an Advanced Product Strategy, Module 1 Lab

## Write your one-page strategy
- **Winning aspiration: define winning in the customer's terms, not internal metrics. What does success look like for the specific person you serve?:** The app is a an active advisor for people's mental wellness.
- **Where to play: segment, geography, and use case, explicitly. Which customers, markets, and surfaces will you focus on?:** Gen Z who are eager to balance life and work, feel need to improve their wellness and are willing to be active sporters but probably do not have enough time to.
- **How to win: your differentiator. What can you do that your specific competitors cannot easily replicate?:** A plan for mental wellness based on current data and predictions linked to client's behavioral habits.
- **Capabilities required: what you must be world-class at. What will you build, buy, or partner for?:** Partnership with Health Insurance companies for affordable packages.
- **Management systems: the metrics and rituals that keep your choices alive quarter to quarter.:** Monthly usage and retention review.
