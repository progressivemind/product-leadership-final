# Craft an Advanced Product Strategy, Module 1 Lab

## Write your one-page strategy
- **Winning aspiration: define winning in the customer's terms, not internal metrics. What does success look like for the specific person you serve?:** The app is a true wellness companion that helps people overcome and monitor their anxiety acute.
- **Where to play: segment, geography, and use case, explicitly. Which customers, markets, and surfaces will you focus on?:** Mozambican urban professionals, aged 40 to 65, experiencing recurring work-related anxiety that interferes with daily functioning.
- **How to win: your differentiator. What can you do that your specific competitors cannot easily replicate?:** Identification of patterns, potential triggers, or contributing factors to anxiety scenarios and recommendations on how to avoid future cases, based on non-payable track history data.
- **Capabilities required: what you must be world-class at. What will you build, buy, or partner for?:** User personalization engine.
- **Management systems: the metrics and rituals that keep your choices alive quarter to quarter.:** Monthly retention review, aged groups with anxiety cases.

## Name your one hard no
- **Your one hard no:** We will not build sports tracking. We will rather integrate selectively with existing activity ecosystems where the data demonstrably improves personalization, because it’s a different business model and my lead to unprecedent outcomes and cost to our customers, given the complexity to integrate different personalization engines.

## Write your three OKRs
- **Objective:** Help users build and maintain healthier mental wellbeing by recognizing rising anxiety early and taking effective action.
- **KR1:** Users who report that personalized interventions helped them manage an anxiety episode: >= 75% by Q1 2027
- **KR2:** Users who take a recommended intervention based on early identification of rising anxiety: >= 45% by Q2 2027
- **KR3:** Reduced recurrence of problematic anxiety episodes: 40% to 10% by Q4 2028

## AI pressure-test
- **Which challenge from the AI is most valid, and why?:** The alignment between the strategic objective and what the OKRs should be (more outcomes and not milestones)
