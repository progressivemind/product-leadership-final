## Write your business case
- **The strategic bet. What specific outcome are you backing, who does it serve, and what is the mechanism that connects the product decision to a financial result?:** Use lightweight, personalized AI check-ins to help post-acute users recognize early changes in their mental wellbeing and take a relevant action before anxiety becomes problematic again.
- **The assumptions. List the assumptions your case rests on, then rank them: which one, if wrong, most changes your conclusion?:** Post-acute users will perceive personalized check-ins as useful enough to return for. Earlier recognition actually increases the probability that users take an intervention. Personalization based on history materially outperforms generic check-ins.
- **The expected return. What does the bet generate and when? Express it at unit level (per customer) and at scale (what volume hits target).:** If the bet increases post-acute retention by 8 percentage points, every 100 post-acute users generate 8 additional retained customers. If each retained customer produces $2 of incremental contribution over the resulting additional lifetime, the bet generates approximately $89 per 100 users, less personalization costs. At 100,000 eligible post-acute users, the same retention improvement produces 8,000 additional retained customers.
- **The kill criterion. Name the specific metric, threshold, timeline, and financial consequence that tells the team to stop. Actionable, not a conversation.:** Stop the bet if, after 90 days of reaching a statistically meaningful post-acute cohort, personalized check-ins do not produce at least a +5 percentage-point improvement in 90-day post-acute retention versus the control group.
